Behind the Lens

Hi, I'm Coral — a Louisiana based photographer with a love for golden light and genuine moments. What started as a hobby turned into a passion for capturing the people and memories that matter most, from engagements to family portraits and everything in between.

My Approach

I believe the best photos happen when you feel comfortable, not posed. My goal is to make every session feel relaxed and fun, so what you get back are portraits that actually look like you — timeless, natural, and full of life.
